# Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The account group is the family's
Neverest carries pimalaya-cli's `account` group over the accounts `-a` names: `account check` is the check, `check` a hidden alias, the report kept multi-account; `account list` prints `{name, default, backends, notBuilt}` rows, `backends` the backend tables the account writes (sources', targets', `smtp`), `notBuilt` those the build leaves out (JMAP always), `default` true on every account. No `account capability`.
