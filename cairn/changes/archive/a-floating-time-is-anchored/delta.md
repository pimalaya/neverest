### Requirement: A floating time is anchored before Google or Graph takes it
Every floating date-time of a document's events is anchored before a Google or Graph write: in the calendar's zone on Google, in UTC on Graph and on a Google calendar stating none, with a warning; the write reports no revision. One scenario.
