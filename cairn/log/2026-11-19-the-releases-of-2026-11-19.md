# The releases of 2026-11-19

io-imap 0.12.0, io-smtp 0.10.0, io-sasl 0.3.0, io-pimdir 0.18.0, pimalaya-cli 0.7.0 and ical-rs 0.7.2, released on 2026-11-19, taken from crates.io, no patch.

- The `imap` and `smtp` features enable `io-imap/std` and `io-smtp/std`, the std runtime having left `client`; `cargo tree` holds io-sasl 0.3.0 alone. neverest calls no `ImapClient::raw` and has no `request` command, so the `Vec<Vec<u8>>` raw reply and pimalaya-cli's `request-origin` move no code. The C5 helpers of io-pimdir 0.18 replace copies in himalaya, cardamum and calendula; neverest has none.
- io-pimdir 0.18 stays storage-, sync- and search-draft-14 (vendoring pimdir e188e31); its `FORMAT_VERSION`, schema and statements equal 0.17's, so a store io-pimdir 0.17 wrote opens as is. neverest holds no sync vector fixture of format 7 or 8.
- pimdir audit-2026-11-12 P5: the retention sweep (src/sync/driver/upkeep.rs `purge_account`) lists the account's expired retained items, then purges them with one `PimdirStore::purge_items` call, one transaction a sweep instead of one an item; a purge failure is warned about, the sweep never failing the run. Guard: src/sync/driver/tests.rs `the_retention_sweep_purges_in_one_transaction` counts the commit frames the store's WAL gains while three items of three collections are purged and another account's is kept: 1, against 3 with the per-item purge.
- N2 (a public in-flight guard on `PimdirSourceStore`) was not released in io-pimdir 0.18: left.

Capabilities moved: sync (*A run reclaims retained items on a schedule*, the opening line).
