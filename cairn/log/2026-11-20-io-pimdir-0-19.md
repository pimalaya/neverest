# io-pimdir 0.19: the one-way target's own edit, the item's own conflict

neverest moves to io-pimdir 0.19.0 (pimdir draft-15), ical-rs 0.7.3 and vcard-rs 0.6.1. A draft-14 store or sync state is refused (`store-version`), never migrated, and the search index is rebuilt.

## What landed

- src/sync/driver.rs: `Authority::Store` (a one-way account's targets) maps to `PimdirConflictPolicy::KeepLocal` where it mapped to `PreferLocal`. Under `PreferLocal` a change made on a target alone was an ordinary pull: it entered the store's item and from there every other target, the source's body never going back. Under `KeepLocal` (pimdir SYNC §5, sync-draft-15) it is a divergence settled for the store: a pushing pass keeps the target's body as a retained item and pushes the store's over it, a pulling pass parks it and `keep_store_bodies` settles it the same way, reported under `overruled` either way.
- The item's own conflict. io-pimdir 0.19 records two endpoints of a two-way account disagreeing as the collection's divergence (pimdir SYNC §9, decided 2026-11-20): under the collection's `manual` policy, the item's conflict (`items.conflict_object`), where it was the second endpoint's binding conflict. neverest read binding conflicts alone, so such a divergence was neither listed nor merged.
  - src/conflict.rs: `Conflict::source` and `handle` are `Option`, `None` for the item's own conflict; `list` appends `PimdirReader::list_item_conflicts`, its base from `common_ancestor`: the `shared_object` (else the sync base) of a binding naming neither side, which the diverging binding keeps pinned. `apply` stages the same `Update` naming the diverging body under `resolves`, drained through the item's first binding, and reads a moved side of an item conflict as `Moved`.
  - src/conflict/report.rs: `source` and `remoteId` are `null` for the item's own conflict, which prints `<id> in <collection>, between its endpoints`.
  - src/sync/driver/reconcile.rs: `merge_conflicts` merges the collection's item conflicts after the side's binding conflicts; `resolve_conflicts` no longer fetches a side's body again for a placement projecting an item conflict whose diverging body the store holds (`binding_conflicts`, `item_conflicts`).
  - src/sync/driver/upkeep.rs: `count_conflicts` counts the item conflicts too, so the run's `outstandingConflicts` and exit 3 see them.
- src/sync/driver/reconcile.rs: `sync_side_rebuilding` prepends the rekey's `PimdirRekeyReport::events` (the `Added` members no old placement resolved to) to the report of the sync that follows, which no longer names them.
- Cargo.toml: io-pimdir 0.19.0, ical-rs 0.7.3, vcard-rs 0.6.1; a temporary `[patch.crates-io]` io-pimdir on ../io-pimdir until 0.19.0 is on crates.io.

`PimdirWritten` and `PimdirLoaded` gained fields; neverest builds neither by a struct literal, so nothing else moved.

## Spec

- [sync](../spec/sync.md): *`one-way` declares authority, not silence* names the targets' `KeepLocal` and the source's `PreferRemote`; *A one-way overwrite keeps the body it discards* covers a target edited alone, with the scenario *A target edited alone*; the rekey requirement and its scenario report the new members as added; *Two endpoints are reconciled with each other* and *A divergence with no common ancestor parks rather than resolving itself* record the item's own conflict and its ancestor; *A conflicted run succeeds* counts it; *A conflict's id prints as a string* prints `source` and `remoteId` `null` for it.

## Tests

- `a_one_way_target_s_own_edit_is_overwritten_and_its_body_kept` (src/sync/driver/tests.rs): fails under `PreferLocal` (the target's pull reports `ContentChanged` and parks nothing).
- `a_renumbered_mailbox_is_rekeyed_before_it_is_listed` gained a message the store did not hold, reported `Added`: fails without the prepend.
- `a_card_both_endpoints_rewrote_diverges_against_their_agreed_body` asserts the item's own conflict is listed (no source, the agreed body its base) and merged, the item conflict cleared; `a_card_two_endpoints_never_agreed_on_parks_with_no_ancestor` asserts it is listed with no base, counted, not merged, then settled by `Conflict::apply`. Both failed on io-pimdir 0.19 before the change (nothing listed, nothing merged).
- The Radicale suites (tests/endpoints.rs, tests/calendars.rs, tests/carddav.rs, `--ignored`) pass against a local container. Their `conflict_id` helpers find a conflict by its local body's `UID` through `conflict show`, an item's own conflict naming no `remoteId`, and the sync report assertions read `remoteId` where they read an `id` the contract audit rulings turned into the store seq earlier on 2026-11-20.

## Left

`--source` names a binding's conflict only: an item standing in its own conflict and a binding's at once is ambiguous without `--source`, and its own conflict is reachable once the binding's is settled.
